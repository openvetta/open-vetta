## Phone navigation

The bottom tab bar is the most copied element on a phone screen: five equal
icons with labels, the active one tinted with the accent, a white bar with a
hairline on top. Drawn from habit it makes every app look like the same app.
Treat the bar as part of the direction, not as a default.

**The default to avoid:** 4-5 equal icon-over-label items on a full-width bar,
active state = accent-coloured icon, one hairline above, nothing else.

**Pick the form from the idea** (and write it into `layout`, one clause):

- **Floating dock** - a pill detached 12-16px from the sides and sitting above
  the bottom 34px, 3-4 items, full radius; the active item is a filled capsule
  behind its icon. Solid, or glass only if the direction has `glass`.
- **Growing active tab** - icons only, and the active one expands into a capsule
  that carries its label. Quiet, compact, one label on screen.
- **Words only** - no icons; the destinations set in the display face, the active
  one heavier or underlined. Editorial and print-led products.
- **Centre action** - two destinations either side of one raised action (scan,
  order, record, pay): a circle or a notch cut into the bar.
- **The bar is the content** - a pinned mini-player, order summary or balance
  strip; destinations sit behind one menu button or a sheet.
- **Top segments, bottom action** - no tab bar: a segmented control under the
  title switches views, the bottom holds the one primary action.
- **Object-derived** - made from the product's own thing: a stack of cards to
  swipe, a dial of roast levels, a tape of dates, a row of seats. Use when the
  destinations are really states of one object.
- **Edge rail** - a thin vertical rail on the thumb side for one-handed apps
  (readers, remotes, camera tools).

**Always:** 56-64px tall plus the 34px safe area (a floating dock clears it); one
icon set; 3-5 destinations, peers only; labels 11-12px when shown; a pressed and
a selected state that differ in more than colour (shape, weight, fill); the active
destination is also named by the screen title.

**Across the three options** the bar must not be the same form twice. If the
nearest competitor's app has this bar, the bar is not yet yours.
